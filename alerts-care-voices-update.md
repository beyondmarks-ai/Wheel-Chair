# Blink Assist 0.16 — alerts, care voices and obstacle demo

## Phone and guardian alerts

Open **Alerts & sounds** on the main screen. It reports notification permission,
blocked categories, missing pop-up/sound settings, guardian configuration and
whether the controller or camera monitoring is running. Each notification category
has a button to open its Android sound and pop-up settings. Test blink and movement
buttons post clearly labelled notifications on this phone only.

Real three-blink emergencies post a local notification and request guardian SMS
when enabled. Real movement notifications arrive over the Bluetooth health feed;
movement SMS must also be enabled in Guardian alerts. Local notification or history
failures no longer prevent the separate movement SMS action. Movement notifications
now use a high-importance category; an explicitly blocked old category stays blocked
until changed by the user. Local movement notices are limited to one per 30 seconds,
and guardian movement notices to one per five minutes.

For the guardian to receive an alert on their own phone, save their number with the
country code in **Guardian alerts**, grant SMS permission, enable guardian SMS and
select the desired care/movement options. The sending phone needs an SMS-capable SIM,
a default SMS SIM, signal and balance. No app installation or internet is required
on the guardian's phone for SMS. Their SMS notification sound is controlled there.
This version does not provision a remote push-notification service.

Manual speaker mode does not run the camera or blink detector. Stop that mode and
tap Start to detect blinks. Keep Bluetooth connected for movement events. Android
silent mode, Do Not Disturb and Vivo background restrictions still apply.

## Custom reminders and natural voices

In **Today & care schedule**, add or edit any reminder. Choose your own time, days
or one date, reminder text, and voice:

| Voice | DFPlayer file | Spoken message |
|---|---|---|
| Tablet time | `mp3/2990.mp3` | It's time for your tablet. Please follow your prescribed schedule. |
| Medicine time | `mp3/2991.mp3` | It's time for your medicine. Please follow your prescribed schedule. |
| Doctor visit | `mp3/2992.mp3` | It's time for your doctor visit. Please get ready for your appointment. |
| My own reminder text | Per-reminder WAV | Reminder, followed by the user's text, generated by Android speech. |

The three preset voices use Sarvam Simran and play offline on the phone. Preview
them in the reminder editor. To use them through the wired speaker, export the
natural voice pack, extract its `mp3` folder onto the existing DFPlayer card, and
mark the matching recording as copied in the reminder. Keep the speaker connected
through either monitoring or manual mode. If wired playback cannot be requested,
the reminder uses the phone voice. A successfully requested but unconfirmed wired
playback is reported as such; it is not claimed to be an audible success.

Custom text still needs an exported recording copied to the card after changes.
The DFPlayer cannot receive newly generated audio through its command-only Bluetooth
connection. Existing schedules and history migrate without being deleted. Changing
a selected voice resets SD readiness. Tracks 2990–2999 are reserved for this pack;
old custom plans that occupied those slots fall back to phone speech.

## Obstacle demonstration

Hold the Blink Assist title to open the hidden panel. **DEMO: obstacle ahead** posts
a demo notification and says **“Demo. Obstacle ahead. Please stop and check the path.”**
It plays on the phone unless `mp3/2999.mp3` is confirmed copied and the wired speaker
is ready. The demo is labelled in history, never invents a distance measurement,
and never sends guardian SMS. It does not repair or claim to test the ultrasonic
sensor. The four existing red voice buttons retain their manual playback behavior.
